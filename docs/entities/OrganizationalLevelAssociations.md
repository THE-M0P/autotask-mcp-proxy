# OrganizationalLevelAssociations

When the Organizational Structure feature is enabled in Autotask, this entity describes the association between an OrganizationalLevel1 (The default term is Division . It may also appear in the UI as Branch, Business Unit, Sector , or Segment .) and OrganizationalLevel2 (The default term is Line of Business . It may also appear in the UI as Application, Core Competency, Function, Line, Offering, Product Line, Product/Service Line, Service Line, Subdivision .)
